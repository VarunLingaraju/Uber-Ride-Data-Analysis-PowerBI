# Uber-Ride-Data-Analysis-PowerBI
"End-to-end Uber ride operations and revenue analytics project featuring an interactive 5-page Power BI dashboard, data cleaning, and business intelligence insights tracking KPIs, vehicle performance, and customer behavior."
## Power BI Dashboard Architecture & Business Requirements

The dashboard is structured into a clean, multi-page interactive report designed to track core ride-sharing metrics, operational efficiency, and customer behavior:

1. **Overview Page:** Features high-level KPIs (Completed/Lost Bookings, Revenue, Total/Avg Distance), monthly and quarterly performance trends, vehicle revenue share, top pick-up/drop-off locations, and driver/rider ratings. Includes a dynamic **Hide/Show Filter Panel** for a clean user experience.
2. **Vehicle Page:** Detailed matrix view showing booking counts, total revenue, and percentage revenue contribution per vehicle type.
3. **Revenue Page:** Deep dive into revenue generation segmented by customer, vehicle type, and payment method across monthly and quarterly timelines.
4. **Rider Page:** Tracks ride cancellations by reason, payment preferences, time-based trends, and customer segmentation (First-Time, Return, and Regular Riders).
5. **Location Page:** Analyzes geographic metrics, including monthly total distances, distance by vehicle type, busy time slots, and high-density busy areas.
